Our Story

About Ellis Tree Service

Seventy years of honest work, fair prices, and taking care of this community — one tree at a time.

Est. 1955

A family business, through and through.

Ellis Tree Service was founded in 1955 and has been family owned and operated ever since. What began as a small local operation has grown into one of Southern Illinois's most trusted names in tree care — but the values have never changed.

We believe in honest work, fair prices, and treating every customer's property like it's our own. When you call Ellis, you're not calling a national chain or a franchise. You're calling a local family that has spent the last 70 years building a reputation in this community.

Our crew has the experience to handle everything from routine seasonal trimming to complex removals near structures, and we back every job with the pride that comes from having our name on it.

We serve all of Jackson and Franklin County, including Ava, Murphysboro, Du Quoin, Carbondale, Benton, Herrin, Marion, and the surrounding areas. If you're not sure whether we cover your area, just give us a call.
