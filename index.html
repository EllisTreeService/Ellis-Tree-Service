Who We Are

A family name you can trust.

Ellis Tree Service has been caring for trees across Southern Illinois since 1955. What started as a family business is still a family business — the same commitment, the same community, seven decades later.

We serve Ava, Murphysboro, Du Quoin, Carbondale, Benton, Herrin, Marion, and surrounding Jackson and Franklin County areas.
