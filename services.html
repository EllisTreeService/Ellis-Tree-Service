Trimming & Removal

Whether you need overgrown limbs shaped back, deadwood removed for safety, or an entire tree taken down, our experienced crew handles it with precision and care.

We work on trees of all sizes — from small ornamentals to large hardwoods — on both residential and commercial properties across Southern Illinois.

  • Crown thinning & raising
  • Deadwood removal
  • Hazardous tree removal
  • Storm-damaged tree removal
  • Commercial lot clearing
  • Debris hauling & cleanup

Service 02

Stump Grinding

After a tree comes down, the stump doesn't have to stay. Our professional stump grinding equipment grinds stumps completely below the surface — no trip hazard, no eyesore, no regrowth.

We grind to the depth you need, whether that's for replanting, lawn restoration, or a clean flat surface.

  • Stumps of all sizes
  • Ground to below surface level
  • Root flare removal available
  • Clean site after grinding
  • Mulch left on-site or removed
